## Decisions that carry forward

- **`_paprika.scss` and `_color-mode-switch.scss` load unlayered** in `main.scss`, after upstream's layered
  blocks and before `_custom.scss`; **the theme's rules that restyle components live in
  `_paprika-components.scss`, which `main.scss` loads inside `@layer vc.app`**. Upstream put every style into cascade layers (VirtoCommerce/vc-frontend#2577), and
  unlayered CSS beats every layer whatever its specificity. The theme's tokens on `:root` / `html.dark`
  and the page font stay unlayered, so they keep winning over upstream's `theme` and `base` layers and over
  its `presets/paprika.scss` (`vc.preset`). Component rules (the lg widget recipe and its zero-specificity
  reset of nested widgets, the configurator option row, `.vc-empty-page`) were written to compete with
  components by specificity; unlayered, the nested-widget reset beat the configurator's own section knobs
  (52px tighter sections, caught by `vc-frontend:visual-compare`, fixed in `da9765dc8`, split into its
  own partial in the next commit, as upstream wires its layered partials from the entry). A new
  component-level rule goes in `_paprika-components.scss`, a token in `_paprika.scss`. Moving the theme into its
  own design folder (`designs/paprika/`: tokens in `theme`, block files in `vc.design`, colours in the
  preset stylesheet) is the upstream-native path, left for a separate task. User (2a); the layer split
  follows from the visual comparison, to keep 2a's "no visual change".
- **VcWidget uses upstream's `seamless`; the fork's `nested` prop is gone.** Only a story used it. Keeping the
  fork's prop would re-conflict on every merge. User.
- **Header counters use upstream's `HeaderBadge` and its placement** (`overlay`: from the icon's centre
  towards the inline end), not the fork's `__badge` positions, which covered the icon. User (screenshots).
- **The account dashboard is upstream's live layout** (VirtoCommerce/vc-frontend#2552); the fork keeps its h1
  visible at every width. User.
- **The root `package.json` keeps the fork's own version** (3.3.0), unchanged from the last log.
